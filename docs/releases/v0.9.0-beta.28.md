Kasumi gets a voice, music and sound. Plus better bug catching behind the scenes.

- New: soft bossa nova music in the menus. It fades out when a game starts. Put your own MP3 or Opus songs in `3ds/kasumi/music` to play those instead. Settings > Audio > Menu music: On, Quiet or Off (X skips to the next song)
- New: Tsumugi (VOICEVOX:春日部つむぎ) welcomes you back when Kasumi opens and sees you off when a game starts. Settings > Audio > Voice
- New: Japanese-style sound effects in the menus (koto, wood blocks, a wind chime, water). Settings > Audio > Sound effects. In a game, only the stream menu and screenshots make a sound
- New: Settings > Controls > Camera stick speed (Slow, Normal, Fast, Fastest) and Invert camera (up-down or both) for the C-Stick
- Game options (game page, X) are redone: the game's name at the top, a dot on what the game changes, "Default (...)" says what Settings uses, and new per-game Camera stick speed, Invert camera and Gyro speed
- Clearer input modes: the lower screen always shows PAD or MOUSE, the POINTER button is now MOUSE, and switching tells you what the buttons do
- "Your game is ready" at the end of a queue is now a temple bell and koto phrase
- Smoother play on slow or laggy connections: Kasumi now waits long enough for a lost piece of video to be sent again, instead of freezing until the next full picture
- If NVIDIA's rig drops the connection in the middle of a game, Kasumi now reconnects by itself (before, the picture could freeze until you left)
- Your login is now kept fresh during long games, so quitting a game from its own menu no longer sends you back to the sign-in screen. Quitting a game still ends its cloud session (as on PC); press A to play again
- The queue counter shows your place in line again on servers that briefly reported a setup step first
- If NVIDIA says your account can't play a game, Kasumi now explains that Steam, Epic and Ubisoft games need that store linked at play.geforcenow.com
- Starting a game again after it ended now counts its play time from zero
- Diagnostics: Kasumi now spots problems by itself (a frozen picture, a stuck screen, an NVIDIA answer it doesn't understand) and, with Share problem reports on, sends a report even when nothing looked wrong to you. Logs are much smaller, so a long session fits in one report
- Fewer pointless reports: no report for a game you don't own or for a normal end of a game

Update from Settings > Updates, or install with FBI (Remote Install > Scan QR Code). Your login, library and settings are kept.
