A touch C-stick on the lower screen, steadier controls on weak Wi-Fi, and clearer errors.

- New: touch C-stick. In a game, tap C-STICK next to PS and the lower screen becomes a camera stick: touch and push to look around, hold it out to keep turning, just like the C-Stick. Double-tap for R3, tap HIDE for your stats again. Settings > Controls > Touch camera switches it to Trackpad (drag to turn) or turns it off, Touch C-stick size picks how far you push, and each game can have its own choice
- Controls on weak Wi-Fi: on a bad connection, input could pause for seconds while lost button data was resent. Kasumi now skips stale controller data and carries on, so the controls stay responsive. Key presses and mouse clicks still always arrive
- NVIDIA busy (limited mode): instead of an error, Kasumi now waits and tries again by itself, with a countdown. It usually gets in within a few minutes
- Clearer "can't play this game": an empty GeForce NOW library or a game that isn't in it now says exactly what to do at play.geforcenow.com, and search marks games that aren't in your library
- 4:3 games like Borderlands 2 no longer fail with "MVD config failed": they show with black bars at the sides
- Signing in with a wrong 3DS clock: Kasumi now warns you on the first screen and says to fix the date and time
- Playing abroad: the "server isn't open to this account" message now says to pick a server in your home country
- Fixed: long error messages ran off both sides of the screen
- Diagnostics: a frozen menu now logs which part of Kasumi was stuck

Update from Settings > Updates, or install with FBI (Remote Install > Scan QR Code). Your login, library and settings are kept.
