GeForce NOW partners (experimental).

- New: Settings > Account > GeForce NOW provider. In some countries GeForce NOW is run by a local partner, with its own accounts and servers: au (Japan), Taiwan Mobile, GFN Korea, Zain, GAME+, StarHub, Digevo, ABYA, Cloud.GG, YES, GFN.AM, rain and bro.game. Pick yours, then sign in: your library, servers and sessions then go through that partner. Auto uses NVIDIA's own recommendation for your country
- If a launch says your account can't stream here, the message now points to this setting
- Nothing changes for NVIDIA accounts: your login stays as it is

Partner sign-in is new and couldn't be tested with a real partner account yet. If you use one, please send a diagnostic report (Settings > System) whether it works or not, so we can fix anything that goes wrong.

Update from Settings > Updates, or install with FBI (Remote Install > Scan QR Code). Your login, library and settings are kept.
