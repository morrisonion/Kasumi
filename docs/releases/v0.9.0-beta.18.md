Fewer failed launches, based on your diagnostic reports. Thank you to everyone sharing them!

- Fixed: launches failing with HTTP 403 ("session limit per device"). A previous session could stay open on NVIDIA's side and block new ones. Kasumi now finds these leftover sessions, closes them, waits until NVIDIA frees the slot and launches
- Fixed: free-tier queues on Server: Auto could freeze at one place for two minutes and then be dropped ("queue abandoned"). When NVIDIA's own choice of server is also the fastest, Kasumi now goes through NVIDIA's normal address, which moves the queue along; Auto still steps in when another server is clearly faster, like on a phone hotspot
- Clearer message when NVIDIA says an account can't stream from where it is ("entitlement failure"), for example in countries served by a local GeForce NOW partner
- Fixed: after being refused, retrying quickly made NVIDIA rate limit the console (HTTP 429). Kasumi now pauses launches for a minute and says so
- Clearer launch errors: "limited mode" (an NVIDIA account restriction), no free rigs, and a session running on another device are explained in plain words
- After a clearly choppy session, Kasumi suggests the Weak / hotspot connection type
- The diagnostic log no longer stops after 6000 lines: it rolls over, so a long session keeps its most recent part for reports
- Diagnostic reports only include Kasumi's own crash dumps, and a finished session's stats are sent even if you close Kasumi right away
