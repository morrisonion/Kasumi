Put your games on the HOME Menu, and a fresh look for every theme.

- New: game shortcuts. On a game's page, tap "+ Add to HOME Menu" (or press SELECT). Kasumi makes a sharp icon from the game's cover and a banner from its official wide art (with its logo), then installs it. A screen shows each step (art, building, installing), then a preview of the real icon and banner and where to find them. Open it from the HOME Menu and Kasumi starts straight into that game, queue and all
- Shortcuts launch from the store you picked on the game's page, with that game's own options. Remove one from the same button, or like any app in System Settings > Data Management
- Shortcuts need Kasumi installed as a CIA (not the .3dsx), and each one uses a HOME Menu slot and about 200 KB of SD card space
- New: ten themes, each with its own wallpaper on the lower screen and frosted-glass buttons and cards that match it. Five are new: Beni (red), Matcha (green), Kaki (orange), Sumi (black) and Shiro (white). New installs start on Ai (indigo); your current theme is kept. Settings > Sound & Look > Theme
- Settings are now six sections (Controls, Picture, Sound & Look, Network, System, Account & Updates): pick one from the grid, L and R step between them, B goes back
- New look: crisp square buttons that press down when tapped, corner marks on buttons and cards, a cleaner game card in the library, brush icons for the Settings sections, and an ensō for games without cover art
- Signing in where a partner runs GeForce NOW (like Cloud.GG in Australia), Kasumi asks which one your account is from, so nobody ends up on the wrong provider. If a game is refused because of it, the message says what to change
- Australia: GeForce NOW there is run by Cloud.GG, and Kasumi now suggests it (NVIDIA's own servers for Australia are in Japan). The provider list also says where each partner is, so "au" now reads "au (KDDI, Japan)"
- Your library refreshes once by itself after this update, so shortcuts get the game's own banner art
- New: screenshot viewer. Settings > System > Screenshots shows the ones you took in games; L and R browse, X deletes
- New: sharpness and colour for the stream picture. Settings > Picture > Sharpness (Off, Low, Medium, High) crisps up edges and small text; Colour (Natural, Vivid, Extra vivid) adds a little saturation and contrast so games look less washed out. Done on the console's GPU while drawing, so it costs no bitrate or frame rate. Starts on Low and Vivid; needs Screen mode: Wide 800. The old "Encoder filter" is now called Server sharpening, and Off is still recommended
- Accurate queue wait: the estimate now comes from everyone's recent queues with your provider (NVIDIA's free queue takes about 2 minutes whatever your place), and from your own when there's no shared data. It was often off by more than a minute
- Fewer frozen pictures after a hiccup: when a burst of video arrives after a network stall, Kasumi now queues it instead of dropping a frame and waiting for the next full picture
- Smoother menus: the top screen no longer tears in half while a list scrolls or a screen animates
- Fixed: covers that sometimes showed only half the picture

Update from Settings > Updates, or install with FBI (Remote Install > Scan QR Code). Your login, library and settings are kept.
