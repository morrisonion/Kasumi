Fewer sign-outs and calmer launch retries, from the beta.22 reports.

- Fixed: Kasumi could sign you out when it renewed your login at a moment it couldn't reach NVIDIA (for example right after waking the console, before the Wi-Fi was back) or when NVIDIA's login server was busy. Your login now stays, and Kasumi tells you it couldn't reach NVIDIA. If NVIDIA refuses to renew a login, Kasumi keeps using it while it lasts, and diagnostic reports now show NVIDIA's answer
- "Session limit" waits are calmer: Kasumi tries again after 1:30, then less often (up to 3 minutes apart, for up to 15 minutes). In beta.22, trying every 45 seconds made NVIDIA refuse launches for "too many requests", while consoles got in after a few quiet minutes
- "Too many requests" (HTTP 429) no longer fails a launch: Kasumi counts down and tries again by itself
- A moment without Wi-Fi during that wait no longer ends it
- When NVIDIA says an account can't stream, the message now fits your country: where NVIDIA runs GeForce NOW itself, it asks you to check the account at play.geforcenow.com (without a VPN) instead of pointing to a partner

Update from Settings > Updates, or install with FBI (Remote Install > Scan QR Code). Your login, library and settings are kept.
