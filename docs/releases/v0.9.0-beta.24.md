Staying signed in, steadier reconnects and clearer messages, from the many reports of October 2nd. Thank you, and welcome to everyone new!

- Fixed: players were asked to sign in again after an hour or two of play. Kasumi now renews its login the way the other OpenNOW clients do, which should keep you signed in. If it still happens, please send a report: it now shows NVIDIA's answer
- Fixed: pressing B while "Closing the cloud session..." showed cancelled the request that ends your game on NVIDIA's side. You saw "session failed", and the game could stay open and block your next launch
- Fixed: Kasumi sometimes thought the video had frozen when it hadn't, and reconnected in the middle of a working game
- Reconnecting after the connection drops now uses the address NVIDIA gives at that moment. Before, these reconnects were often refused (HTTP 503)
- "Session limit" waits: NVIDIA limits how often one console can start games, especially for free accounts. The wait screen now says so (it usually clears in 2-5 minutes, and Kasumi keeps trying), and Kasumi gets a fresh session slot more often while it waits. Letting the wait run is the fastest way in
- If the server you picked isn't open to your account ("region not supported"), Kasumi switches Server to Auto and tries again by itself
- "Limited mode" is now explained as what it usually is: NVIDIA limiting new sessions for a few minutes
- When a free session reaches its one-hour limit, Kasumi says your free hour is over instead of "session failed"
- On a slow or far-away connection, Kasumi suggests Weak / hotspot mode or a closer server once per game

Update from Settings > Updates, or install with FBI (Remote Install > Scan QR Code). Your login, library and settings are kept.
