Smoother streams and fewer launch problems, from your reports and testing.

- Fixed: regular hitches and lots of re-sent packets on a good connection. The 3DS kept doing background Wi-Fi work (StreetPass, SpotPass and notification checks) during games, which pulled the radio away for a fraction of a second at a time. Kasumi now takes the Wi-Fi for itself while a game runs, like Moonlight does, and gives it back when you leave the game. In testing, lost frames dropped from about 1.5 to 0.4 a minute
- Fixed: after one choppy session, a network could stay in Weak / hotspot mode for two weeks. A smooth session now switches it back to Standard
- Fixed: resuming a game that was still running (on the 3DS or another device) connected to an old address and failed with "session ended"
- A session left open by an older Kasumi version can't be closed from the new one; only NVIDIA can end it. Kasumi now says so clearly and keeps retrying for up to 12 minutes instead of saying the session had ended. This only happens once after updating from beta.18 or older
